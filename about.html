About Smart Campus Energy Sentinel

A client-side green computing framework for collegiate computer application laboratories

1. Project Background & Rationale

Collegiate institutions house hundreds of personal computers across practical laboratories, digital libraries, and server rooms. Because timetables alternate between theory classes and practical sessions, workstations sit unattended for 3 to 4 hours daily at an active desktop, continuously dissipating 50W to 65W of unutilized electrical load per terminal.

The Campus Energy Sentinel was developed as a BCA Semester I initiative to bridge this visibility gap, transforming unmonitored IT facilities into measurable, energy-optimized environments.

2. Key Academic Objectives

  • Empirical Measuring: Quantify active and idle power consumption in kilowatt-hours (kWh) across collegiate hardware profiles.
  • Fiscal Accountability: Model unmitigated monetary waste in Indian Rupees (₹) based on commercial state electricity board tariffs.
  • Carbon Accounting: Convert electrical waste into atmospheric carbon metrics (kg CO₂) using official Central Electricity Authority (CEA) baselines.
  • Automated Power-Cycling: Introduce actionable timetables aligned with ACPI S0 (Active), S3 (Suspend to RAM), and S5 (Soft-Off) power states.

3. Software Architecture & Technology Stack

The system executes entirely on the client side without external runtime or server requirements:

HTML5: Semantic UI Structure & Accessibility
CSS3: Modern Glassmorphic Dark-Slate Theme
JavaScript (ES6): Deterministic Measuring Engine
